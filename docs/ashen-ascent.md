# Ashen Ascent

A souls-like example game for Over the Edge that places **every one of the 59
set pieces** in the Workshop's library exactly once. They lie along one
continuous climb through eight zones, from a graveyard at ground level to a
castle drifting in the sky, with its summit at **about 407 m**. It is a complete
[project](projects.md) made only of data: an ash-grey theme seen through a perspective
camera, scenery from every model in the [decoration library](decorations.md), HUD,
synthesized music and sound cues, pixel-art crows and hollow soldiers, messages, secrets
and its own course.

The course is generated by [`scripts/ashen-ascent/`](../scripts/ashen-ascent), and
the committed [`examples/projects/ashen-ascent`](../examples/projects/ashen-ascent)
is that generator's output. The [full map](ashen-ascent-map.svg) is drawn from the
same geometry.

## Play or release

```sh
GAME_PROJECT=examples/projects/ashen-ascent npm run dev:game
GAME_PROJECT=examples/projects/ashen-ascent npm run build:game
npm run preview:game
```

Deploy the release as described in the README's
[game-only release](../README.md#game-only-release) section. To open the game in the
Workshop, build a Workshop with it
(`GAME_PROJECT=examples/projects/ashen-ascent npm run build`; see
[publishing a Workshop with its project](projects.md#publishing-a-workshop-with-its-project)),
which also lists its level first under **Workshop / Level / Server levels**. Or copy the
directory into the project server's folder (`cp -r examples/projects/ashen-ascent projects/`)
and open it from **Workshop / Project**.

Each zone has its own palette, and pieces take their colours from it. The
connectors between pieces change from zone to zone rather than repeating one
pattern. Stairs switch back or run in line, mixing shelves, slabs, crates, rocks and
columns; floors, walls, towers and wind stones fill the rest. The first zone opens
with the intro. Each later zone begins at a **bonfire**, a blade planted in warm ash,
whose popup names the zone the first time you arrive. Bonfires are landmarks, not
checkpoints. As in any Over the Edge course, falling never kills you; you just land
lower down.

## Fair falls

Every fall lands on an earlier part of the route. Mountain masses are solid, upper
walkways float over earlier ground, and walls at the edge of the world stand flush
against the pieces beside them. The generator checks this before it writes anything.
Under its reach model, the ending must still be reachable from every foothold the
player can reach, so the course has no pits you cannot climb out of. Falling below the
lowest terrain restarts the run, as in every level.

## Surprises

*Spoilers follow.*

- **Lying messages.** "DO NOT RIDE SNAKE" guards the only road, which rides the
  snake; the message above it reads "LIAR AHEAD". "TRY FALLING" at the Ossuary's
  brink is true: the way on is down. "SOLID GROUND AHEAD" marks the false floor, and
  a hidden draft in its pit lifts you back out.
- **The one honest swamp.** In the mire, "TRY THE SWAMP" is true. Beside the
  stepping stones, the mud is an illusion over a grotto. Its hidden draft carries you
  47 m straight up, through the Catch wall's chasm to the Forge catwalk, and skips the
  rest of the mire and the lower forge. Raise or tuck the hammer so it does not catch
  on the opening.
- **The false summit.** At 272 m a flag, and "THE TOP, SURELY", claim the top.
  Behind it the wind altar's **Great Updraft** throws you 96 m up past drifting islets
  to the keep's sky dock.
- **The mimic.** In the courtyard, "TREASURE AHEAD" points at a golden chest. The
  chest and the flagstones under it are illusions over the undercroft, where a draft
  sends you back up.
- **A view of nothing.** "GREAT VIEW AHEAD" leads along a balcony to a turret that
  goes nowhere.
- **Ambushes.** A hollow waits on the keep's roof where the ledge ladder tops out,
  a crow guards the gilded stair, and crows circle the drawbridge. The crumbling ice
  bridge says "DO NOT LINGER" and means it.

## Scenery

190 decorations, using all 26 models of the decoration library, dress the climb from
1,000 m behind the course to 3 m in front of it. None of them collide: every prop is a
decoration, from the graves by the path, the bonfires and the heap of skulls in the
catacombs to the stalactites, roots and icicles hanging below the route, the banners
of the keep and the islets drifting past the Great Updraft. The only colliders are the
course itself, so the pot never catches on a prop:

- **Ashen Hollow:** graves, an iron fence, pillars, a knight statue and a lantern post in
  the graveyard. Behind it stand dead trees and a broken gothic arch, rows of rock shelves
  with a watchtower on the farthest, and a viaduct. Mountain ranges and the golden great
  tree line the horizon.
- **Hollow Hamlet:** lantern posts, banners and crumbling walls along the street, a cage by
  the upper lane, and a cathedral far off.
- **The Ossuary:** a dark crypt wall with skull piles, candelabras and a statue before it,
  and chains and cages hanging in front of the climb.
- **Blighted Mire:** drowned trees and thorns on mossy ground, a rune obelisk, a sword
  grave and far crags.
- **Cinder Forge:** braziers on the catwalk, an ember cairn, chains and a cage overhead.
- **Frostbound Ramparts:** banners and knight statues on the walls, and a frost-blue range
  and watchtower in the distance.
- **Windward Stair:** crags adrift in the wind, and chains and a banner on the tower.
- **The Drifting Keep:** candelabras, lantern posts, knights at the gate, and a cage and
  chains below the courtyard. Far below lies the golden tree.

A second castle floats far behind the course and foreshadows the keep. From the
graveyard only the crags hanging beneath it show, at the top of the view. It floats
level with the forge, and by the keep it lies below the climb. Distant scenery is
placed by where it should appear on screen from a point of the climb, so it drifts only
slowly past. The graveyard's background stands on rows of rock shelves. The theme hides
its backdrop hills, because the scenery is the landscape, and pushes the fog out to
1,500 m, so the far ranges read as silhouettes.

## Budget

| Objects | Count | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Terrain | 399 | Including 10 illusions; blocks, ramps, triangles, circles and hexagons only |
| Updrafts | 16 | 13 marked, 3 hidden; lift 5-96 m |
| Other triggers | 15 | 14 popups (11 with a sound) and the ending, which stops the timer |
| Enemies | 34 | 23 crows and 11 hollow soldiers |
| Labels | 16 | The level limit |
| Decorations | 190 | Scenery only; all 26 library models |

That is 654 objects plus the start. The course uses the five built-in shapes, so
all terrain shares **5 geometry templates** and no custom polygons. In the release it
renders in **46-65 draw calls** (8,000-23,000 triangles) along the climb, about half
of them scenery, and only nearby enemies run physics. Idle frames upload nothing for
the scenery. The project's 11 WAV files, about 1.2 MB in total, are synthesized by
`ashenAscentMedia()` in [`scripts/project-fixtures.mjs`](../scripts/project-fixtures.mjs).

## Regenerate

```sh
node scripts/ashen-ascent/generate.mjs            # write the project and the map
node scripts/ashen-ascent/generate.mjs --preview  # also write zone crops and a reach overlay to artifacts/ashen-ascent/
node scripts/ashen-ascent/generate.mjs --check    # fail if the committed files differ from the generator
```

Change the generator, not `level.json`: `npm run verify:project` runs `--check`,
then builds the example as an editor-free release and opens it. The generator places
each piece with the library's own `placeSetPiece`, so part IDs follow
`<piece>-<zone>-<part>`. Before it writes anything, it checks all of the following:

- every library piece is used exactly once;
- no connector overlaps a piece's parts or intrudes into its footprint;
- enemies start clear of terrain;
- the course's own drafts rise through open air up to their apex;
- no two small colliders (1.5 m or less on their longest side) lie within 1.2 m of each
  other, touching included, except parts of one set piece: a narrower slot traps the
  pot or the hammer head;
- the ending is reachable under conservative reach rules (2.35 m hammer pull, 2.5 m
  rise, 1.9 m hops, drops up to 9 m);
- nothing reachable is a trap;
- the level and the whole project pass the engine's validation.
